One VM placement, around the whole loop

The placer reads capacity from Postgres, locks a node and creates the VM. The VM's events and the node's new requests then travel through the collectors, the gateway and Kafka back into Postgres. Only when the metrics show the new VM does the placer let go of the lock.

How long the node stays locked

The lock is held until a metrics snapshot taken after scheduling reaches Postgres. That makes the scrape interval the main cost: about 25 s in the lab (15 s scrape), up to about 70 s on OpenShift (60 s federation). Placements on other nodes carry on in parallel.

Times are typical for the configs in this project: 5 s collector batch, 2–5 s gateway batch, 15 s (lab) or 60 s (OpenShift) metrics interval.
